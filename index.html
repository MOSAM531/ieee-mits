What is IEEE MTT-S?

The IEEE Microwave Theory and Technology Society (MTT-S) is a global technical society dedicated to advancing the theory and application of RF, microwave, millimeter-wave, and terahertz technologies. MTT-S fosters research in 5G/6G, radar systems, satellite communications, and biomedical wireless applications worldwide.

🏛️

About MITS-DU Gwalior Chapter

The IEEE MTT-S Student Branch Chapter at MITS-DU Gwalior provides a collaborative platform for students of ECE, EEE, CSE, and allied branches. We organize hands-on technical workshops, simulation hackathons, guest lectures from scientists, and project mentorship to help students build real-world engineering skills.
